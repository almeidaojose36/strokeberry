## Intent

A ~20-second vertical launch ad for Strokeberry. Concept chosen from the pitch round:
"#2 Guess the drawing, with #4's ending". A sketch builds line by line while the text asks
"Guess what it is 👀"; a color reveal lands on a drum beat; repeated three times, faster each
time. The close is "the ad draws itself": the Strokeberry logo and "Try it free" draw in stroke
by stroke, then snap into brand color. Playful, satisfying, sells by showing the product working.
Sell (promo), not a site tour.

## Customizations

- Music bed with each color reveal landing on a beat.
- Sound effects: soft pencil-scratch under the drawing, a "pop" on each reveal.
- Countdown ring ticking before each reveal.
- Real exports play sped up (~2×–3.5×) so three reveals fit.
