# Design
Concept: a miniature drawing lesson turns a simple outline into a character or scene, then invites the viewer to create with Strokeberry.
Paper #FFF8EC, charcoal #1B1B1B, red #C81E2A text / #EC2D34 accent, deep teal #0096A0. Bricolage Grotesque bold headlines and Figtree captions. Artwork is focal, top brand rail and lower numbered instruction anchor the frame. Solid paper background, bold red rule. Preserve full mascot exactly; scale only.
Motion: spring-pop-entrance on title and mascot; linear progress rule. Two scenes: continuous lesson, then closing card. Deliberate silence allows social-platform music selection.
