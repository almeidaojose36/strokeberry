# Strokeberry 30-day social plan (Instagram + Facebook)

**Audience:** United States (and Canada). **Times (US Eastern):** 9:00 AM carousel · 12:30 PM Reel · 7:30 PM Reel.
In Business Suite the times are in Africa/Johannesburg: 3:00 PM · 6:30 PM · 1:30 AM the next day.
Posted with Meta Business Suite to both strokeberry.app (Instagram) and the Strokeberry Facebook page.

Reels and carousels are built by `scripts/social/build_posts.py` into `marketing/social/posts/<id>/`: Reels have a
designed first frame (Instagram uses it as the cover) and an original track from `marketing/music/`. US spelling.
Lengths are mixed (14 to 40 seconds) so we can see in Insights which holds attention best.

**Every day (you):** 20 minutes replying to comments and leaving genuine comments on US cafés, interior designers,
realtors, teachers and small brands. That is the biggest growth lever and can't be scheduled.

## Notes from the first run
- Posting through Business Suite: type captions with a trailing space after the last hashtag (an autocomplete otherwise
  replaces it), and click Next/Schedule with a real mouse click, not a scripted one, or the caption isn't saved.
- The kitchen Reel went live early without a caption; the Instagram copy was fixed. The Facebook copy shows
  "Failed to publish" after an edit attempt: check it in Business Suite > Content.
