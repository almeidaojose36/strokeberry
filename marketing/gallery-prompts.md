# Strokeberry starter gallery — image prompts

Prompts for the in-app "Start from an example" gallery. Generate them yourself in the image tool of your
choice, then drop the files in `marketing/gallery-source/` (I'll wire them into the app).

**Before you use any image:** check that your image tool's plan allows **commercial use** of what it generates
(users will make videos from these, and we'll show them in marketing). Don't generate real brands, logos,
celebrities or existing cartoon characters.

## Output spec (applies to every image)

- **Square, at least 1024 × 1024** (1536 or 2048 is better). PNG preferred.
- **Plain pure-white background**, subject centred, generous empty margin (about 10–15%) on every side.
- **No text, letters, numbers, watermarks, signatures or borders** (AI text comes out garbled and draws badly).
- One clear subject; avoid tiny details and photographic textures — Strokeberry draws **outlines**, so bold,
  clean shapes produce the most satisfying videos.
- File name: the ID in the list, e.g. `animals-01-fox.png`.

## Style suffix — paste this after every prompt

> Clean vector-style illustration with bold, smooth, dark outlines of even weight and flat, bright colours.
> Friendly, modern, slightly playful. Pure white background, subject centred with generous margins.
> No shading gradients, no texture, no shadows, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border.

For the **tutorial sheets** (category 8) use the tutorial suffix at the end of that section instead.

| tutorial-05-flower | A how-to-draw tutorial sheet of a potted tulip. |
| tutorial-06-fish | A how-to-draw tutorial sheet of a round tropical fish. |

**Tutorial suffix — paste after each tutorial prompt:**

> Arranged as a 2 by 2 grid of four equal square panels on a pure white background, separated by white
> space, no divider lines. Panel 1: only light grey basic circles and guide lines. Panel 2: clean black
> outlines of the subject. Panel 3: the outlines plus details and small features. Panel 4: the finished
> subject in flat bright colours. The subject is the same size and in the same position in every panel.
> Clean vector style, no shading gradients, no texture, no text, no numbers, no watermark.

**Total: 50 images** (8 + 7 + 6 + 7 + 7 + 4 + 5 + 6).

Tips:
- Generate 2–4 variations per prompt and keep the cleanest one — simple, closed outlines win.
- Test a few in the studio before generating everything: upload one, press play, and check the drawing looks good.
- Avoid anything resembling the Strokeberry mascot (a strawberry with headphones) — that's reserved for the brand.
