00:00 Upload your artwork
Click New project, choose your image, and see it appear in the app preview.

00:10 Choose your settings
Select Ink, enable the drawing pencil and color, then choose 9:16 portrait and HD quality.

00:20 Set up drawing steps
Review the 2 × 2 crops and the top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right order. Set each stage to 15 seconds, then prepare.

00:34 Preview your drawing
Use Play or select a completed stage. The exported result excerpt is shown at 4× speed.

00:43 Export and download
Click Export video. When rendering is complete, choose Download MP4.

00:54 Create with Strokeberry
Save this walkthrough and create your own drawing video.
