# Showcase batch 01: 12 clips for TikTok, Reels and Shorts

All clips are 9:16, 1080×1920, about 15 seconds. They are drawn like a Pro export (no watermark) and end on the
"Made with Strokeberry · strokeberry.com" card. **They are silent on purpose:** add a trending sound in the app when
you post (it helps reach). Every picture is our own artwork: no brands, no famous characters.

**How to post each one**
1. Upload the MP4. Use the matching `-cover.jpg` (the finished picture) as the cover where the app allows it.
2. Add the **hook** as on-screen text for the first 3 seconds, in the empty space at the top. Use the app's own text tool.
3. Paste the **caption** and pick a trending sound.
4. Reply to early comments: the **question** at the end of each caption is there to get them.

**Links in bios** (so the analytics show which platform works):
- TikTok: `strokeberry.com/?ref=tiktok`
- Instagram: `strokeberry.com/?ref=instagram`
- YouTube: `strokeberry.com/?ref=youtube`

Where a post can carry its own link (Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit), use the clip's own link below.

**Posting order (one a day, two on days 1 and 7):** 10, 01 · 05 · 03 · 07 · 11 · 09 · 12, 04 · then 02, 06, 08.
Lead with the "guess what" clips: they are the easiest to watch to the end.
