Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Strokeberry respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google or an email link we receive your email address and, for Google, your name. Sign-in is handled by Google’s Firebase Authentication.
  • Your images and videos. The images you upload, the projects you create and the videos you export are stored on our servers so you can reopen projects and download your videos.
  • Payment information. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy. We never see or store your card details; we receive only your subscription status.
  • Technical data. Our servers keep basic logs (such as IP address and errors) to keep the service secure and working.
  • Anonymous usage counts. To see how Strokeberry is used (for example how many people visit, export a video or open the upgrade offer) we count page visits and a few steps in the studio on our own server. We don’t use cookies or third-party analytics for this. Visitors are counted with a code made from the IP address and browser and a random value that changes every day and is then deleted, so we can’t recognise you from one day to the next and don’t store your IP address for this.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your data or show ads.
  • We don’t use your images to train AI models and we don’t send them to third-party AI services.
  • We don’t use advertising trackers.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser’s storage only for things the service needs, such as keeping you signed in and remembering your settings. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when a page loads.

Who helps us run Strokeberry

  • Google (Firebase Authentication) for sign-in.
  • Lemon Squeezy for payments and tax.
  • Our hosting provider, where your files are stored.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your projects and videos until you ask us to delete them. Email support@strokeberry.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account and files within 30 days. Guest projects that are never linked to an account may be removed after a period of inactivity.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. If you are in South Africa, this includes your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA); if you are in the EU or UK, your rights under the GDPR. Email us and we’ll help.

Children

Strokeberry is not for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions: support@strokeberry.com.
