FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kinds of images work best?

Illustrations, line art, logos, and drawing tutorials with clear outlines on a plain background give the cleanest strokes. Photos work too, but produce busier drawings. The built‑in cleanup tool helps with photographed paper.

Is this AI?

No generative AI is involved in making your video. Strokeberry uses computer‑vision techniques to find the lines in your image and plan a natural drawing order. Your artwork is never sent to an AI service.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. Open the studio, pick one of our examples or upload your own image, and watch it draw. You only create a free account when you want to export a video, and it takes a few seconds with Google or an email link.

What happens after my 3 free videos?

You get 3 new free videos every month, counted over a rolling 30 days, so each one comes back a month after you made it. You can keep your projects and preview as much as you like in between. To export more sooner, get a video pack (from $5, paid once, never expires) or go Pro: $10 a month, or $84 a year (about $7 a month). Free exports are 1080p with a small watermark and a short “Made with Strokeberry” end card; packs and Pro remove both, and Pro adds 4K.

What is a video pack?

A one-time purchase of 5, 15 or 40 videos for people who don’t need a subscription. Pack videos never expire, have no watermark or end card, and can be up to 5 minutes in 1080p. If an export fails, the video goes back into your pack.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Free videos are for personal use. Videos made with a video pack or with Pro can be used commercially: social media, ads, client work and product promotion. You must own or have the rights to the image you upload.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from the billing link in the studio and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. See the refund policy for the details.

How long can a video be?

Free videos can be up to 1 minute. Pro videos can be up to 5 minutes. In step‑by‑step mode you set the time for each stage, and the total counts toward the same limit.

Does it add audio?

Not yet. Videos are exported silent so you can add a trending sound or voiceover in Instagram, TikTok, or your editor.

What does the drawing hand look like?

Choose a realistic hand in a light, medium or dark skin tone: it draws every line with an ink pen (or a pencil, in the Pencil style), then picks up a brush and paints the colour in. You can also use a simple pencil, or no hand at all for a clean, self‑drawing look.
